Customer Implementation – Smart Parking Management System (Top 10)

1. User Registration & Login
Customers can create accounts and securely log in using authentication mechanisms.

2. Profile & Vehicle Management
Users can manage personal details and add/edit multiple vehicles.

3. Location-Based Parking Search
Customers can search nearby parking spaces using GPS/location services.

4. Real-Time Slot Availability
System displays available parking slots dynamically based on selected area and time.

5. Parking Slot Booking
Users can select location, time, and duration to reserve a parking space.

6. Advance Payment System
Partial payment is required to confirm bookings through a secure payment gateway.

7. Booking Confirmation & Notifications
Users receive instant confirmation and reminders via app/SMS/email.

8. Booking History Management
Customers can view past and upcoming bookings with full details.

9. Cancellation & Refund
Users can cancel bookings and receive refunds based on system policy.

10. Rating, Feedback & Fine Handling
Customers can rate parking services and pay fines for violations if applicable.
